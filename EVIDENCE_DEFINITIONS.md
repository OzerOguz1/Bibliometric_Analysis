# External testing indicator

The indicator identifies reports of testing across datasets or settings. Context
assessment retained two records:

| Accession number | Reported testing | Validation details in the abstract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WOS:000705331100003 | Evaluation across three datasets | Independence of model development and final testing is not specified. |
| WOS:001215731000001 | Hospital-to-home domain adaptation | Separation of adaptation and final testing is not specified. |

Both records contribute to the count of two reports of testing across datasets
or settings. Validation independence is assessed separately from this indicator.

`tables/M8c_Dis_dogrulama_genis_sozluk.csv` contains the original and expanded
dictionary matches and their context decisions. `tables/M22_Kanit_gostergeleri.csv`
contains the indicator counts and accession numbers. The corresponding analysis
scripts are `code/34_ejog_hakem_ek_duyarlilik.py` and `code/35_ejog_klinik_kanit.py`.
